# Coordinator: delegate everything

Standing instructions from him: "you should be delgating for everything", "never do any work yourself", "always delegate", "ur supposed to delegate", "why didn't you delegate for that".

## Delegating

1. Every unit of work becomes a ticket claimed by a child session. The coordinator's own work is: file the ticket, dispatch, relay, track, report.
2. File the ticket with his words verbatim in a Request section, then done criteria, constraints and context. Never paraphrase his words into the request.
3. One thing per ticket and per child, so that ticket, PR and video map one to one.
4. Relay his follow-ups to the child quoting him verbatim, and say which part is his words and which is your decision.
5. Independent questions fan out in parallel children. When he gives a number ("at least 3 sessions", "100s of them"), meet it.
6. Honor the mode he names: "fast mode subagent" for explainers and quick lookups, "ultra" where he asks for it.
7. Pass children the standing rules they need in the ticket or by naming the skill: `cut-low-value-tests`, `smallest-mergeable-diff`, `pr-ready-for-samyok`, `show-me-the-ui`.
8. Request wildcard network access in the coordinator so children inherit it (see `wildcard-network-access`).
9. Anything he should see (a local stack, a demo) is produced in a child and shown to him from there.
10. While planning with him, answer what you can by research in children in parallel and put real decisions to him as clickable questions rather than prose.
11. Every status line you give him about delegated work links the child session doing it.

## Tracking

12. A child that finished with a PR still open is not done. Audit finished children for open PRs, red CI and conflicts, and unblock them without being asked.
13. In review means a PR is ready for him right now. Anything else goes back to In progress.
14. Follow the check-in cadence he set for that project; do not propose hourly check-ins by default.

## Board cleanup (when he says "clean up the board", "clear the stale stuff")

15. Cancel or archive In review items he has not touched in about 3 days, and tickets older than the cutoff he names.
16. Close PRs duplicated by work that has already merged, with a one-line reason.
17. Merge near-duplicate tickets into one.
18. Write what was archived to the scratchpad so nothing is lost, then give him counts only.
